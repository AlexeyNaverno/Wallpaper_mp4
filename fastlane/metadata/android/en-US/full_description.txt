Set an MP4 video as a looping, silent live wallpaper and let Android derive a system palette from it.

The app samples five frames from the selected video, calculates primary, secondary and tertiary colors with Android WallpaperColors, and supplies them to the live-wallpaper service. When the selection changes, the service notifies Android so it can request the updated colors.

Videos are selected through Android's system document picker. The app keeps access only to the selected file, does not copy it into app storage, and has no network permission. Playback goes directly to the wallpaper surface; the app does not cache the complete video in memory.

Requires Android 8.1 (API 27) or later and MP4 codecs supported by the device. Applying dynamic colors depends on the ROM and SystemUI.
